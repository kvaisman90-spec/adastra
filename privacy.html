PRIVACY POLICY

AdAstra Platform
Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to AdAstra ("Platform", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our advertising platform at adastra.app. The Platform is operated by AdAstra Platform Operator, based in Israel.

2. DATA WE COLLECT
We collect only the minimum data necessary:
- Account name (chosen by you at login)
- Email address (only during payment for receipt delivery)
- Advertising content (images, videos, text, CTA, contact info)
- Payment transaction records (amount, date, method — no card details stored)
- Opt-in subscription status (explicit consent flag)
We DO NOT collect: passwords, government IDs, physical addresses, phone numbers (unless voluntarily provided as ad contact), or browsing history.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
- To display your approved advertisements on free platforms and to opt-in subscribers
- To process payments via crypto/on-ramp/PayPal
- To communicate about your account or ads
- To comply with legal obligations

4. OPT-IN SUBSCRIPTIONS
Advertising is sent ONLY to users who have explicitly consented ("opted in"). Users may unsubscribe at any time. We never send unsolicited messages.

5. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY
Data is stored on Supabase servers (EU/US regions). All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Crypto payments are processed via NOWPayments/Cryptomus; card-to-crypto conversion is handled by their secure on-ramp. We do not store payment credentials.

6. THIRD PARTIES
- Supabase: database hosting
- Vercel: frontend hosting
- NOWPayments / Cryptomus: crypto payment processing & card on-ramp
- PayPal: payment processing
No data is sold or shared with advertisers or brokers.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
Under Israeli law and GDPR (where applicable), you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing kvaisman90@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. CHILDREN
The Platform is not intended for users under 18.

9. CHANGES
We will update this policy as needed. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
